The KPI tracking UI is already built. I need you to build the backend scoring system and wire it into the existing UI.
Context: SolarPak is a solar nonprofit with 6 teams. Each week, team leaders log their progress. The system calculates a score (0–100) per KPI, a team score (weighted average of KPI scores), and an overall SolarPak score (average of all team scores). All targets are Year 1 targets. Weeks elapsed is tracked automatically from a start date set in settings.
The 6 teams, their weekly inputs, and scoring formulas:
1. Operations & Installations
Inputs: families_served_to_date, completion_rate_pct, uptime_failures (integer), co2_reports_submitted (int), co2_rounds_completed (int), install_reports_published (int), install_rounds_completed (int)
Scoring: families → (actual / (40/52 × weeks)) × 100; completion → (pct / 90) × 100; uptime → uptime_failures === 0 ? 100 : 0; CO₂ reports → (submitted / rounds) × 100; install reports → (published / rounds) × 100
2. Fundraising & Donations
Inputs: funds_raised_to_date, retained_donors, total_prior_donors, new_funding_sources, cost_per_family_last_round
Scoring: funds → (actual / (10000/52 × weeks)) × 100; retention → (retained / total_prior) × 100; sources → (actual / 1) × 100 capped 100; cost → (120 / actual_cost) × 100 capped 100
3. Marketing & Outreach
Inputs: follower_count_start (set once), follower_count_now, avg_monthly_reach, media_mentions_to_date, donor_conversion_pct
Scoring: followers → ((now - start) / start / 1.0 × 100) scaled to 100 at 2× growth; reach → (actual / 5000) × 100; mentions → (actual / (2/52 × weeks)) × 100; conversion → (pct / 2) × 100
4. Volunteer Management
Inputs: active_volunteers, tasks_on_time_pct, meetings_held_pct
Scoring: volunteers → (actual / 30) × 100; tasks → (pct / 80) × 100; meetings → (pct / 85) × 100
5. Impact Labs
Inputs: surveys_completed_pct, reports_published, reports_due, co2_documented_rounds, total_rounds, energy_saving_pct
Scoring: surveys → (pct / 70) × 100; reports → (published / due) × 100; CO₂ → (documented / total) × 100; energy → (pct / 20) × 100. Institutional funding target is 0 for Y1 — auto-score 100.
6. Events & Community Outreach
Inputs: events_hosted_to_date, attendee_growth_pct, fundraising_raised_to_date
Scoring: events → (actual / (4/52 × weeks)) × 100; attendees → (pct / 20) × 100; fundraising → (actual / (5000/52 × weeks)) × 100
Rules for all scores:

All scores capped at 100
All scores floored at 0
Team score = average of its KPI scores
Overall score = average of all 6 team scores
Scores 80–100 = green, 50–79 = amber, 0–49 = red
Store each weekly submission with a timestamp so progress can be tracked over time
weeks_elapsed is calculated automatically: Math.floor((Date.now() - START_DATE) / (7 × 24 × 60 × 60 × 1000)) + 1, where START_DATE is configurable in a settings panel
Display each team's score as a progress ring or bar, and show the overall score prominently at the top
Each KPI row should show: input field, calculated score, and RAG status indicator
Add a history tab showing weekly score snapshots per team over time as a line chart

Wire this scoring engine into the existing UI. All calculation logic should live in a single scoring.js utility file so it can be tested and updated independently.
